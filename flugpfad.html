✈️ Flight Path Viewer

Load a flight log and see your flight as an interactive 3D map — with terrain, attitude display, metrics and a timeline. Works with an EdgeTX / OpenTX telemetry log (.csv) and with a raw INAV / Betaflight blackbox log (.txt / .bbl / .bfl), which adds airspeed, wind, G-force, HDOP, baro temperature and more. Everything runs directly in your browser; the file is never uploaded or stored.

Drop a log here or choose a file
EdgeTX telemetry CSV, or a raw INAV / Betaflight blackbox log (.txt / .bbl / .bfl). GPS telemetry required.
What you get
🗺️

3D map

The flight path over real terrain and satellite imagery, free to rotate and zoom.

🛩️

Attitude display

A 3D model shows roll, pitch and yaw at every moment of the flight.

📊

Metrics

Altitude, speed, climb, distance, battery, link quality — read automatically from the log. Blackbox logs add airspeed, wind, G-force and more.

⏱️

Timeline

Scrub through the flight on the band, toggle extra curves on and off.
