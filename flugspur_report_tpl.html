Every function and control at a glance

Control the map

Left dragPan the map.
Right dragTilt & rotate – horizontal turns the view, vertical tilts between oblique and top-down.
Mouse wheelZoom.

Under View → Control you can set the pace of zoom and rotation to Fine, Normal or Fast.

Timeline (bottom)

The band shows the flight over time. The filled area is the metric chosen under Color; up to three more curves lie on top.

Clickjumps to that moment.
Dragselects a time range – only that range is then shown on the map and in the metrics.
▶ / Spaceplay and pause; set the speed next to it.

Range opens exact start/end entry; Curves chooses which extra lines appear on the band.

Search

Finds extreme values (highest / lowest of a metric) or every moment at a specific value – with tolerance, greater or less than. Hits appear as marks on the path and in the list.

Each hit moves the time cursor: the spot shows on the timeline as a numbered mark and the metrics on the left jump to it. Click a hit in the list to return there.

With a time range selected, hits outside it are greyed out in the list and hidden on the map.

Marks

Drops data points along the path – automatically, at fixed time or distance spacing. Labels can stay visible or appear only on hover.

Measure

Click to place points on the map and measure distances and areas. Points can be dragged; bring the chain back to the first point to close an area.

Battery

Pick a battery profile (cell count, capacity). Flugspur then computes state of charge from current and used capacity and shows it as a bar.

View

Mapbackground: satellite, satellite with labels, OpenTopoMap, plain map or dark.
Text sizescales the interface; the button on the right resets to 100 %.
Controlsensitivity of zoom and rotation.

Camera follows model locks the view to the aircraft; Altitude wall to ground draws height as a vertical wall. Below you can reset the view or save a map image.

Attitude display

Top right, the aircraft model turns with the real attitude from pitch and roll.

Drag modelrotates the viewing angle – horizontal around the vertical axis, vertical tilts between side and top view.
Double-clickrestores the front view.

The buttons Front, Rear, Side, Top and Oblique set fixed views; the active angle is highlighted.

Start & reference point

The START or LANDING marker shows the reference point detected on the ground. If the log already begins in the air it reads LOG START at the height of the first sample. Distances and heights are relative to this point.

If the recording ends on the ground, a green LANDING marker sits at the end of the path. What matters is where the log stops – a clean standing ground phase need not be present, since telemetry often cuts off at disarm. If the recording ends in the air (a log fragment), there is no landing point.
