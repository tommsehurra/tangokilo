What you're looking at

🛰️ Live view

While the aircraft is flying, the page shows live telemetry: speed, distance and altitude up top, a vario bar, a compass and an artificial horizon, plus battery, current, consumption and remaining flight time. It switches here automatically the moment a flight starts.

📈 Altitude profile & radar

The altitude profile tracks height over the last minutes. The radar shows the aircraft relative to the start point only — no real coordinates are ever exposed.

🏆 Records & last flight

When nothing is flying you see the all-time records across every flight and a summary of the last flight with its height profile. These values are the same for everyone.

📸 Gallery

A slideshow of FPV photos runs at the bottom. Click an image to enlarge it; use the arrows or dots to move through, or swipe on a phone.

🗺️ Map Downloader

Under Tools, download offline map tiles for the SatMap widget on your radio and generate the Lua widget — pick an area on the map, write it straight to the SD card. Nothing leaves your browser.

✈️ Flight Path Viewer

Under Tools, upload your own EdgeTX log (CSV) or a raw INAV / Betaflight blackbox log to see it as an interactive 3D map with terrain and attitude. It runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded or stored.

📡 Channels

Twitch is where the flights run live; YouTube has flights to rewatch. Both are linked under Channels.
